test tone plays a short beep on the ticked jacks, at a fixed level. identify jacks plays each jack on its own: 1 beep headphones, 2 line out, 3 ear out.

The mic goes in the headphones (combo) jack and needs a 4-pole phone-headset (TRRS, CTIA) plug. A 3-pole TRS mic won't record there: its plug connects the mic to the headphone output instead. Use a TRS-to-TRRS mic adapter.
